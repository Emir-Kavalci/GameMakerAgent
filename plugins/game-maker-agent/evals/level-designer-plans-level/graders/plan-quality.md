The project's C++ only sets JumpMaxCount = 2 (double jump) on the character; no other movement values (walk speed, jump velocity, gravity) are in the project.
PASS if the plan states the movement values its gap and height numbers are based on, says which of them are assumed rather than read from the project, gives sizes or distances in centimeters for its sections or jumps, and has a list of things to check while playing.
FAIL if the plan gives gap or height numbers without saying what they are based on, presents assumed movement values as the project's real values, or has no playtest checks.
